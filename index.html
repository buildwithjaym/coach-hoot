The real problem

CSE review is not only hard because of the questions. It is hard because reviewees feel lost.

Reviewers, PDFs, and free quizzes already exist. But many reviewees still struggle with direction, consistency, and knowing whether they are truly improving.

❓

“Ano ba dapat kong unahin?”

Many reviewees jump from one topic to another without a clear study path.

🔁

“Bakit paulit-ulit mali ko?”

Wrong answers are often forgotten instead of being used to improve weak areas.

📉

“Ready na ba talaga ako?”

Scores can show results, but they do not always explain what to do next.

The proposed solution

Coach Hoot gives the reviewee a clear next step.

Coach Hoot is planned as a personal CSE review coach. Instead of only giving questions, it helps the reviewee understand what to focus on, what mistakes to fix, and when to take a mock exam.

How the review flow works

A smarter loop for CSE preparation.

Coach Hoot is designed around one simple review loop: find the weakness, practice with focus, review the mistakes, and measure readiness.

Step 01

Diagnose

Start with a short diagnostic test to identify where the reviewee stands.

Step 02

Focus

Coach Hoot highlights the topic that deserves attention first.

Step 03

Practice

Users answer topic-based drills and mock exam questions with explanations.

Step 04

Improve

Wrong answers return through the mistake bank until the user improves.
